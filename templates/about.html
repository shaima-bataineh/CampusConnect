About CampusConnect

Bridging the gap between campus and career

We built CampusConnect to make it effortless for students to discover opportunity, and for companies to reach the next generation of talent.

Our Mission

Making campus opportunity accessible to everyone

CampusConnect started with a simple observation: students were missing great opportunities because event information was scattered across flyers, emails, and outdated portals. Companies, meanwhile, struggled to reach engaged students efficiently.

Today, CampusConnect brings career fairs, workshops, hackathons, and networking events into one clean platform — with direct messaging so conversations don't get lost after the event ends.
